### Project Summary

* Team 201 is developing a modular, multipurpose aquatic ROV designed primarily for inspection and monitoring of surface and shallow water conditions, intended for applications such as ship hull inspection, coast patrols and marine wildlife monitoring. This datasheet covers subsystem A3, a bluetooth low-energy link built around an ESP32 microcontroller to provide wireless communication with the remote control unit. Subsystem A3 is a standalone PCB unit capable of connecting to other susbsytems via 8-pin ribbon cables for data and power transmission. A 12V battery can be connected to A3 for power distribution to the ROV's other subsystems.
* Part of group 201's [team report.](https://egr314-s-2026-201.github.io/)
